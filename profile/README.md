# XIM Audio

Licensed music production data for AI training: multitrack stems printed from the producer's own session, the DAW sessions themselves, MIDI, presets and more. Every creator accepted terms that name AI training.

- **[datasets](https://github.com/ximaudio/datasets)**: the measured numbers of every set, licence tables for 40 open music, DAW project and MIDI datasets, a stem-set checker and a delivery loader
- **[Dataset cards on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/ximaudio)**
- **[Datasheet on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23883162)**
- **[Request a sample or a quote](https://ximaudio.com/request?ref=github)**

No audio is hosted here. The data is licensed per set, non-exclusive, on request.
